A CSV exported from the Neurosity console. Eleven columns, no header row, 256 rows a second. A five-minute recording is about 13 MB and takes a moment to read.

Your recording is read inside this browser and never uploaded. There is nowhere to upload it to.

Reading the recording…
Band power, signal quality and the indicators are computed here from the raw signal, using the console's own band edges and its own 1 Hz drift removal. The indicators are not Neurosity's focus and calm scores, which are model outputs a recording does not contain. Nothing here is diagnostic. Screenshots and terminology refer to the Neurosity console (Neurosity, Inc.); this is an independent interpreter's guide, not a Neurosity product.
